# Brag Plan: Celestial Blade

## What is this app?
An editorial, cinematic 3D scrollytelling dark-fantasy web experience where a 3D astral dagger tumbles continuously through tropospheric realms into ancient bedrock, featuring live flight telemetry, pointer-following aerodynamics, interactive blade aiming, and an interactive 3D PBR Blade Forge customizer with 360° free orbit inspection.

## The angle
A cinematic web trailer treating a frontend development project like an AAA dark-fantasy release. Highlighting the intersection of WebGL engineering (Three.js PBR shaders, procedural starfields), GSAP ScrollTrigger scrollytelling, real-time procedural Web Audio synthesis, and editorial typography.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"14,200 METERS ABOVE BEDROCK."
A dramatic altitude readout descending at terminal velocity, followed by the gold-gradient hero reveal: "FALL INTO THE UNKNOWN."

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **Interactive Line of Sight**: "Aim the blade into your eyes" — demonstrating the trigonometric pointer tracking and straight-to-camera tip lock.
2. **Live Flight Telemetry**: Altitude dropping, atmospheric speed climbing to 840 km/h, core friction superheating to 1,400°C.
3. **The Blade Forge**: Opening the PBR customizer drawer — 4 Damascus metallurgy finishes, runic luminescence shifts, and free 360° orbit rotation.
4. **The Impact**: Terminal acceleration and seismic bedrock impact.

## Outro / punchline
"THE DESCENT IS OVER. YOUR CODE WILL NEVER BE THE SAME."
Brand card: **CELESTIAL BLADE** &middot; Three.js + React 19 + GSAP

## User flow worth showing
1. **Descent**: Scrolling through cosmic heavens as real-time altitude telemetry counts down.
2. **Interact**: Holding click to lock razor tip into direct line of sight.
3. **Forge**: Customizing Damascus steel finishes and rotating 360° in 3D.
4. **Impact**: Bedrock crash with sub-bass seismic resonance.
